# headspace-study — iteration log

One entry per authoring iteration: what changed in the package, why, and which gate or critic note it answers.

## 0.1.0-rc.1

First package from the approved analysis (name, tags and ten rules as approved): five signature recipes (sunny color fields with bulging edges, flat shape kit with crescent shading / sparkles / spot plates / confetti, breathing glyphs with the pop-dots loader and choice row, chunky pills on a 2px ledge with the dot tab, product vignettes on color), Never list, Interaction and Motion tables, Applying the concept for 16 contexts, twelve components specified. Validate clean; bundle about 19,950 of 20,000 tokens.

Decisions and departures from the analysis:
- Accessibility: text on tangerine is ink (`color.onField.orange`), not white (white measured 2.7:1, under even the 3:1 large-text bar); error red deepened to 5.3:1; form-control borders use a derived warm grey at 3.2:1 (`color.border.strong`) while row and list borders keep the reference's softer warm grey; secondary text a warm grey at 6.8:1 (the measured muted grey was cool).
- Ink is `color.action.primary` (every main action) and blue is a separate `color.action.accent` (one conversion action per region), so agents reaching for "primary" get the reference's dominant ink pill.
- Arc direction stated as "the field bulges out into the white page" (sag below a top band, rise above a closing band and footer) instead of convex/concave, which read ambiguously.
- One large field per viewport defined as a band or a panel wider than half the content; dashboards skip the header band and let a row of 3–4 category stat tiles be the field; utility areas take the cream field.
- Input focus is a 2px `color.focus.ring` border instead of an outline (the analysis derived blue `#0061ef`; the focus blue keeps one focus color system-wide). Dark focus ring is light yellow on navy.
- Dark mode derived from the night palette: navy page, indigo panels, ink pills turn white with ink labels, night bands inside a night page turn purple, the cream field turns indigo.
- Left out to stay within budget and keep rules unambiguous: the arc draw-on and marquee (one-offs on marketing pages) and the blurred gradient clouds (the analysis allowed them as optional; Never forbids gradients outright for clarity). Mascot faces, the single-dot mark, copy and artwork excluded per the analysis.
- Items without an image get a cover in their category hue with an original shape composition (general rule, so grey boxes and picture icons never appear).

## 0.1.0-rc.2

Answers evaluation 1 of rc.1 (signature 3, needs 4), from author brief 1. Bundle about 19,930 of 20,000 tokens: the new rules were paid for by tightening wording (Motion paragraphs that repeated their recipes, component state rows that repeated the Motion table, "Token mapping: as in States" lines, Verification phrasing), no rule or check removed.

- Item 1, generic and repeated pictograms (major, signature): new **Pictograms** rule in the shape kit — name the subject in one noun, build that noun's outline from 2–3 crescent-shaded primitives so it reads without the title, dots/rings/plain discs are accents only, 60–70% of plate or cover with a tucked sparkle, neighbours must differ in silhouette, pose or supporting shape (a hue swap is not a new drawing), empty states get a full composition or a large glyph. Item covers now point to it (was "2–3 shapes at 50–65%, varied by rotation or hue"). New-project step asks for pictograms of subject nouns; Verification checks covers name their subject and neighbours differ. Holds for any product: a CRM's contact groups, a docs site's sections and a booking flow's room types all need recognizable, distinct pictograms.
- Item 2, header-band art scattered and pie-split (major, signature): header bands now take one **cluster** (lead crescent-shaded disc or blob at 60–80% of the art box, second shape in a second hue overlapping 10–20%, sparkle within 8–16px, fully inside the band, layout varied per area), with an SVG recipe; the art box is inset and centered in the trailing third instead of clipping at the band edge. Crescent shading stated as one hue plus its own shade, never two flat halves; two-color discs limited to confetti. General: every header band in any product.
- Item 3, live data built as a vignette (major, spec-caused): removed the dashboard activity card from the vignette's places; Limits now say a vignette is an illustrative fragment and real records (feeds, lists, tables) render at once and in full; Motion bars builds on feeds; the dashboard row says the stat tiles are the one saturated field and other panels are cream (indigo at night) and as tall as their content. General: any screen with real records.
- Item 4, item image moved to the end on phones (major, spec-caused): Layout adaptation now separates content images (an image or cover picturing the item — a product, a listing, a profile, a document) that stay right after the title, from decoration that moves last. General: any detail screen.
- Item 5, row glyphs unrelated to labels (minor, no principle given): one clause in Breathing glyphs → Where, "its form chosen for the row's label", covered by the same subject-noun idea; no further change.

## 0.1.0-rc.3

Answers evaluation 1 of rc.2 (signature 3, needs 4), from author brief 1. Bundle about 19,970 of 20,000 tokens: room made by tightening wording (vignette What/Motion/glass chip, Pictograms paragraph, eight hue classes reduced to one example plus the list of hues, redundant "Token mapping: as in…" lines in text field, tabs, table and navigation, a duplicated reduced-motion sentence in Accessibility, the dialog's destructive variant now points to Button), no rule or check removed.

- Item 1, invented records in empty states (major, spec-caused): removed "an empty state as a 40%-opacity preview of the filled screen" from the vignette's Where. The Empty state row now says: never sample records, names, prices or totals, real-looking or faded; one shape-kit composition of the area's subject or a `size.glyphLg` glyph, title, one line, one ink pill, on an area-hue panel or cream when a field is in view. Verification checks it. Holds anywhere: an empty inbox, an empty booking list or an empty search in a docs site must not show fake data.
- Item 2, header-band art a small repeated stamp (major, signature): the cluster is now 3–5 overlapping shapes (large lead shape chosen for the area's subject — sun, moon, blob, hexagon, pill, cloud — a medium overlapping second shape, sparkles in two sizes including a 32–64px four-point star), filling the band's trailing 40–50% and at least `size.art` tall on desktop (header band min-height follows), no two areas sharing a lead shape and layout; disc-with-cloud named as one option, not the default. The recipe example changed from disc + cloud to hexagon + pill + two sparkles so the example stops being copied as the default. Sparkle range in the shape kit widened to 16–64px. General: every product with more than one area has header bands.
- Item 3, header band and item cover stacked as two fields (major, hierarchy): Color fields Limits now say one saturated field per viewport, two fields never share a hue and are at least `space.8` of white or cream apart, and a detail screen whose item image or cover is wider than a third of the content gets a cream (indigo at night) header band with the hue only on nav dot, tag and cluster. Applying the concept and Verification updated. General: any detail screen with a large image (a listing, a document, a profile).
- Item 4, phone header bands growing with a stacked art row (major, spec-caused by "decoration moves last"): Layout adaptation separates body decoration (follows the content) from the header band, which below `breakpoint.md` is no taller than its text + `space.8` above and below + the arc, with a 72–96px cluster absolute in the top corner, text kept clear, never its own row or a column; CSS recipe added. Navigation: phone top bar one row (wordmark, primary pill, menu circle), links and avatar in the menu. General: every product screen on a phone.
- Item 5, thin small-scale art (minor, no principle given): not a reason for a candidate; partly covered by the empty-state rule above (one composition of the area's subject). Logged as variance. Also replaced "a goal as a ring and dot" in Imagery, which contradicted the Pictograms rule (rings and dots are accents only).
- Item 6, loose front-page vignette (minor, no principle given): left alone, logged as variance.

## 0.1.0-rc.4

Answers evaluation 3 of rc.3 (holdout review; signature 3, needs 4), from author brief 3. Last allowed candidate: everything that passed is kept. Bundle about 19,910 of 20,000 tokens. Room came mainly from renaming the primitive color group `color` → `palette` (the convention other packages use): the bundler only lists `color.*` in the semantic tables, so the 44 raw palette rows no longer repeat in both light and dark tables; agents keep every semantic token and `tokens.css` still carries the raw values. Plus small tightening (Layout adaptation's compact-header line now points at the recipe). No rule or check removed.

- Item 1, header-band art a small sticker, one sample copied piece for piece (major, signature): art is now sized by width — the art box is `min(42%, size.bandArt)` of the band (new `size.bandArt` 640px), its SVG `width: 100%; height: auto` in a 400×240 viewBox, and the header band's min-height follows that width. The cluster is a scene of 4–5 pieces spread across the box: a lead shape that pictures the screen's subject in one noun (key or open door, a group of discs, a coin stack, a calendar page), a second-hue shape cutting in by 10–20%, a 48–64px star, a small sparkle, optional cloud; front pages prefer a product vignette with confetti. The single hexagon sample is replaced by three samples with different silhouettes (tall centre, wide low row, diagonal rise), labelled structure only, with "reusing a sample's shapes or arrangement is a copy" and "art narrower than a third of the band, or a disc and pill that picture nothing, is a sticker". Verification updated. General: every product with more than one area opens its screens with header bands; one example invites copying, several varied ones plus a construction method do not.
- Item 2, bordered white plan cards (major, spec-caused): the Card variant was the spec's own contradiction of the no-border card rule. Now an **option card** (plans, tiers, packages, any pick-one set): `color.surface.subtle`, `radius.lg`, no border or shadow; name as eyebrow, figure at `font.size.h2` bold tight; selected = the set's one saturated field with on-field text and check disc, in a hue clearly apart from the ground. `radius.md` no longer lists plan cards; Verification checks option cards. General: any pick-one set of cards (subscription tiers, room types, shipping speeds).
- Item 3, night band purple in dark with a violet selection on it (major, spec-caused): `color.field.night` in dark now aliases a new `palette.night.band` deep navy (a step off the navy page) instead of purple; Color use says it stays navy, never purple, and adds that a highlight on a field takes a clearly different hue in both appearances (yellow, blue, or white with ink on a dark band), never a neighbouring hue; Never list adds "a saturated panel on a field of a neighbouring hue". General: any highlighted item on any colored band.
- Item 4, no-results state shown with the loading glyph (minor, no principle given): one clause each — pop dots mean loading only; the Empty state row adds no-results: a pictogram of what was searched and a pill to clear the query or filters. Cheap and general (any search or filter); otherwise minors are not a reason for a candidate.
- Item 5, glyphs unrelated to their labels (minor, no principle given): one clause in Breathing glyphs Limits (a glyph pictures the label beside it; a control repeated on every page keeps one glyph and hue) and in the Toast/confirmation row (a success composition is all about the finished thing). Cover fill under 60–70% is already a rule the agents missed: variance, no change.

## 0.1.0-rc.5

Answers evaluation 2 of rc.4 (signature 3, needs 4), from author brief 2. Final round: everything that passed is kept. The main fix corrects rc.4's own rule instead of adding a new one: a band's height comes from its content and the art fits the band. Bundle 19,996 of 20,000 tokens. Room came from writing `aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"` once instead of on 11 SVGs, condensing the glyph cycle selectors into one comment, dropping the unused `size.bandArt` token and the vignette-only person/system dot classes, and tightening wording (Verification, Applying rows, rule Why clauses, Color fields Where/Limits, shape-kit Motion/Limits). No rule or check removed.

- Item 1, header bands sized by their art (major, hierarchy, spec-caused by rc.4): removed the art-derived `min-height` and the `size.bandArt` token. A header band is its text plus `space.10`/`space.8` (about 200–260px on desktop, under a third of the first screen). The art box sits absolutely in the trailing 40%, `height: 100%; width: auto; max-width: 100%`, in a 400×200 viewBox (all three samples redrawn), so it spreads sideways and never sets the height. Color fields Limits: a band's height comes from its content, and only a front-page hero (display headline, lead, pills) grows around its art. Layout adaptation: on a screen whose job is an action, the first control, the primary action and its feedback sit in the first desktop viewport. Verification updated. General: every product screen in any product opens with a band that names the page, and a form, checkout or settings group needs its controls above the fold.
- Item 2, loose stack of vignette cards (major, signature): Product vignettes rewritten as one scene around one anchor (a device frame, a cover tile, or a blob-cut panel with an offset second-hue edge, about 60% of the box) holding a real-size UI fragment. At most two chips overlap its edge by about a quarter of their size, and a 48px-plus star and a cloud touch it. Nothing floats free, no lone dots under 16px, never more than two same-shaped cards. New CSS recipe (anchor + `::before` offset edge, chip--a/b, star, cloud, build in order). The header-band cluster now points to it instead of "white cards overlapping". General: any front page, onboarding or feature tour.
- Item 3, phone heroes and stat tiles over a screen tall (major, hierarchy, spec-caused by "card grids to one column"): Color fields Limits now say that below `breakpoint.md` no field exceeds one screen and decoration never adds height. The vignette shrinks to a 140px anchor by the pills, or gives way to 2–3 confetti at the band's edge, and never becomes its own column (CSS included). Layout adaptation exempts stat tiles from the one-column rule. Card: stat tiles have no min-height, and on phones they stay one group (2×2 with the plate dropped, or a sideways-scrolling row) in half the first screen. General: any marketing hero or KPI row on a phone.
- Item 4, uneven pictograms (minor, no principle given): one clause in Pictograms says that on one screen each spot pictures its own noun (the band's subject is not reused in a plate or empty state). The fill and recognisability rules already exist; the remaining misses are agent variance.
