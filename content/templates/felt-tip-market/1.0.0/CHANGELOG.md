# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First release of Felt-tip Market: a cheerful storefront drawn in black felt-tip. Paper ground, 1px ink hairlines and
4px corners, flat poster-color bands that meet at ink seams, chunky ink-outlined sticker objects and one big friendly
grotesk. Buttons lift onto a hard black shadow and slam flat when pressed. A dark theme comes with it.

- Ten defining rules and a Never list: pure ink borders on every product surface, no soft shadows or gradients,
  color only as whole flat fields, one band color per product area, and pink kept for the one action that matters.
- Hard-lift buttons: hover lifts 4px onto a solid ink shadow, press lands flat; the main call to action can stack
  over two colored plates, once per screen.
- Sticker objects built by recipe: a face swept down into a solid edge with one ink outline, sized for heroes,
  header bands, empty states and media boxes, with a reusable SVG builder. Items without their own image get a
  drawn vignette of their subject, never a stock picture glyph.
- Poster color bands for headers, split checkerboard sections and a closing band; front pages open on a hero with
  a large headline, a ring of stickers and a call to action paired with an input at one height.
- Ink-hairline panels, tables and forms with pill markers for "where you are", a notched price flag, and a dark
  theme with a black ground and raised panels one step lighter, where bands and stickers keep their colors.
