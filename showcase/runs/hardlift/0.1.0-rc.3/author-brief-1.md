## What to fix

Critic findings that fail the candidate:

1. **major**, componentStates, 1 of 1 sample(s)
   - Principle to fix: A linked card's hover is the lift. A fill change never replaces it. Put the .lift class (or its exact hover rule: translate -4px -4px plus shadow.md in color.shadow.hard) on the card's outermost bordered element, the one that receives the pointer. Any background tint is optional and only comes together with the lift. Put the card's hover in the Interaction table as 'Linked card | lift, as buttons'. In the final check, hover one card in each theme and confirm that its position and shadow changed. A color change alone fails the check.
   - What was seen (for understanding only): Linked product cards still don't lift on hover. The hovered card gets a pale fill on its text area only. It doesn't move -4px and gets no hard offset shadow, in light or dark, so it sits flush with the rest of the grid. The hard lift on linked cards is a signature element. This is unchanged since the previous round, even though the spec now has a 'Linked cards' paragraph and a verify step.
   - Evidence: `showcase/runs/hardlift/0.1.0-rc.3/commerce/20261019-125720/screenshots/state-card-hover-desktop-light.png`, `showcase/runs/hardlift/0.1.0-rc.3/commerce/20261019-125720/screenshots/state-card-hover-desktop-dark.png`
2. **major**, signature, 1 of 1 sample(s), caused by the spec
   - Principle to fix: Give every menu, popover, select and disclosure trigger an explicit open state, [aria-expanded="true"], that wins over hover: color.surface.raised fill with the ink border (or color.action.inverse for a solid trigger), no pink, at a selector specificity at least as high as the ghost hover rule. Add an 'Open trigger' row to the Interaction table. Exclude triggers from the ghost button's pink hover: a trigger's hover shows the ink border only. Verify by opening every menu with the pointer left on its trigger and checking that nothing pink appears besides the screen's one main action.
   - What was seen (for understanding only): The account-menu trigger is still solid pink while its menu is open, in light, dark and mobile. That makes a second loud pink element and breaks the one-pink-action rule. The spec bans a pink open-menu trigger, but its own ghost-button recipe ('.lift--ghost:hover fills pink') makes any ghost trigger turn pink while the pointer rests on it after the click. The Interaction table also has no open-trigger row.
   - Evidence: `showcase/runs/hardlift/0.1.0-rc.3/dashboard/20261019-125720/screenshots/state-user-menu-open-desktop-light.png`, `showcase/runs/hardlift/0.1.0-rc.3/review-1/sheets/dashboard--state--user-menu-open--desktop-dark.png`, `showcase/runs/hardlift/0.1.0-rc.3/review-1/sheets/dashboard--state--user-menu-open--mobile-light.png`
