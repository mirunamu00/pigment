## Reasons

- critic blocker: The save-confirmation toast lands on top of the form's action row. On desktop it hides most of the pink 'Save changes' button and its label, and its pink close button adds a second pink control. At phone width the bottom-pinned toast covers the lower half of both Save and Reset. This is a regression: review 1 of this version showed an inline 'Saved' check that covered nothing.
- critic blocker: At phone width, once the page is scrolled, the scroll-bound hero drift moves the coin, star and speech-bubble stickers up over the pink 'Read the featured post' CTA. The coin covers most of the label, so the page's main action becomes unreadable and partly hidden. The keyboard-focus capture at the top of the page shows no overlap, so the overlap depends on scroll position.
- critic blocker: At phone width, the sessions table reflows into stacked blocks, but each block's Revoke button collapses to a box about two letters wide. The label spills past both sides of the border, so the row action is broken and hard to hit. This shows on the security page and in its password-mismatch state, in light and dark. It is a regression from review 1 of this version, where every Revoke button was a proper button.
- critic major (1 of 3, spec): While refreshing, the band's action button turns into a borderless pale-gray box with pale-gray 'Refreshing…' text that is barely readable on the teal band, in light and dark. The spec's generic disabled recipe (subtle fill, disabled text, hairline border) conflicts with its own ban on disabled-looking text on a band or colored fill and with the 4.5:1 floor. The other samples keep the ink or paper button with a turning coin.
