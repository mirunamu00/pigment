> Review: 138/138 checklist items by 8 reviewer(s) and a lead reviewer (showcase/runs/felt-tip-market/0.1.0-rc.1/review-1).
> The blog hero, with its lemon poster band, big grotesk headline, black CTA and edge-cropped objects, and all four apps' product chrome (header bands with black seams, black and white current pills, 1px ink frames on paper, notched pink flags, dashed empty states, hard-shadow lift, black footer) clearly speak the reference's language, and the four apps read as one family. What keeps it below the reference's craft is the stickers. They are thinner, smaller and flatter than the reference's chunky extruded coins, and in the product apps they shrink into corner fragments. Generic grey and stock image placeholders, a dark mode whose grey ground and black panels reverse the reference's (the spec's own tokens set this up), and loose phone navigation also count against it.

| gap | major | signature | 1 of 1 | The stickers are thinner, smaller and flatter than the reference's. The reference's coins are big die-cut objects about 200px across, with a deep extruded rim, and they dominate the band corners. Here the blog hero's objects are mid-size line drawings with thin rims. The spec's dark tokens set it up this way: surface.default is #242423 and surface.raised is #000000. |
