> The product chrome in all four apps reads clearly as the reference's language and as one family: lemon, periwinkle, teal and marigold header bands closed by black seams, black and white current pills, 1px ink frames on paper, notched pink flags, dashed empty states, the hard-shadow lift and the black footer. Two blog samples also build a real poster hero. What the reference is best known for is still far off: its big, chunky extruded stickers come out as thin line drawings on the hero and as cropped corner fragments in every product app, so the signature score drops below the bar this round. Since the earlier verdict, nothing named there has closed.
> Two problems run through every app: dark mode is inverted (grey ground under black boxes), a spec bug in the dark surface tokens, and missing images become stock glyphs on grey. Both make large parts of the output look generic next to the reference. Phone pill rows and tables are also looser than the reference's tidy phone screens, but those are the agents' own layout faults and do not lower the scores.

| gap | major | signature | 3 of 3 | The sticker language is still well below the reference in every app, and the earlier verdict's main gap has not closed. The reference's stickers are large die-cut objects, about 200px, with a deep extruded rim, and they shape the composition at the band edges. Here the hero objects are mostly 100–150px flat line drawings with little or no back face. Every product app has a single header-band sticker of about 60–100px, pushed into the band's top corner and cropped by the side and the top seam, so on desktop it reads as a fragment and on phones only a sliver shows. Of the settings stickers, only the billing token has a real side. The empty-cart drawing reuses the header bag, recoloured. One blog sample has no front-page hero at all, only a compact header band with one cut-off object. |
| gap | major | referenceFidelity | 3 of 3 | Dark mode reverses the reference's depth on every screen of every app. The page ground is dark grey (#242423) and every card, panel, table, menu, dialog, input and empty box is pure black, where the reference has a black ground with boxes a step lighter. Panels sink instead of rising. The black top bar and footer merge with the panels. Hard shadows (35% white) barely show, so menus, dialogs and the hover lift lose their picked-up depth. Dashed frames fade, and an ink-outlined sticker inside a black box loses its outline. In the same way, the status pill on a colour band turns black in dark because it is filled with `color.surface.raised`. The spec's own dark tokens set this up, and it has not changed since the earlier review. |
| gap | major | polish | 3 of 3 | Content without its own image falls back to a generic picture glyph (sun and mountains, or a photo icon), usually on a flat grey fill. Every product card and product page in commerce does this, and so does every article figure in the blog, where two different subjects share one identical glyph. The reference never shows stock glyphs or grey. Empty media is white with a dashed ink line and a small solid glyph of the thing, and every drawing is a distinct ink-outlined object. Here it is the most visible generic element on the content-heavy screens. |
| gap | minor | componentStates | 3 of 3 | Several states fall short of their recipe. A successful save shows only a tiny dot and one word instead of the white ink panel on the hard shadow. One billing sample leaves a gap below the plan cards about twice the other panel gaps. The reference keeps one edge and even gaps between panels. |
