> Review: 75/75 checklist items by 4 reviewer(s) and a lead reviewer (showcase/runs/felt-tip-market/0.1.0-rc.4/review-3-holdout).
> The language carries to both new products. Each hero has a lemon band, a 96px headline, a black CTA beside an outline button, and thick ink-outlined stickers on their own subject cropped at the edges. The dark inversion matches 11 (pink CTA, unchanged bands), and a careful eye would take the home screens for the reference team's work. The docs chrome (compact bands, ink-framed code, pill tabs, dashed empty states) is close to 06/07.
> The gap from the reference is poster ambition beyond the home hero. The marketing pricing and customers pages open on compact product bands, and the home's middle sections fall back to generic icon-card and stat-strip forms. Pricing is where 02 is loudest, so the selling page reads as a settings page.
> Craft is good but not yet the reference's: the sticker ring is light in places, dark-mode edges are soft, phone header and table handling is crude, and dense tables are unevenly proportioned. One spec rule (band min-height from sticker size) directly causes empty band space.
