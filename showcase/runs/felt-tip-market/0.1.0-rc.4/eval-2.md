> Review: 138/138 checklist items by 8 reviewer(s) and a lead reviewer (showcase/runs/felt-tip-market/0.1.0-rc.4/review-2).
> The front pages read as the reference's own work: paper and lemon bands, a 96px headline, a black CTA beside a bordered field at one height, thick ink-outlined stickers cropped at the edges, the black current pill and the full-height header block, and a faithful dark inversion. The same family holds across all four apps.
> The gap is on product screens. Header bands are padded to a 200px sticker the spec requires and stay empty, where 06's band is compact and full of controls. Pink flags fill whole table columns, which the spec's 'one per row' wording invites. States, phone rows and dark-mode edges are cruder than the reference's hairline controls. Fixing the two spec-caused rules should lift spacing and polish clearly.
