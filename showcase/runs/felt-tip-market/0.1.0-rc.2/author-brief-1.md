What keeps it from the reference's quality:

1. **major gap from the reference**, signature, 1 of 1 sample(s)
   - Principle to fix: Before you finish, measure every sticker as it renders. On a desktop hero, at least half the stickers are 180–260px across, and none is under 120px. On a phone, the largest are at least 120px. A header-band sticker is at least as tall as the band's title block plus lead, and at least 60% of it stays visible. Crop it on one edge only, never into a corner where half of it is gone. On every sticker, the side band between the face and the back is a solid strip of the same fill at least 10% of the object's height (about 18px on a 180px object). If a sticker reads as a flat outline with a sliver under it, push the back lower and enlarge the object. Card and figure vignettes use the same depth.
   - What was seen (for understanding only): The stickers exist now across all apps: a ring around each front-page hero, one object per header band, and one vignette per card and figure. They are original and on-topic, but they still don't have the reference's scale and heft. Hero objects are mostly 100–150px on desktop and about 100px on phones. Most show a side band only a few px deep, a sliver about 5% of their height, so they read as flat outlines with a lip. Several band stickers (the overview parcel, the about-page notepad) are pushed into a top corner with half the object off-screen, so they read as fragments. The reference's coins are 200–260px thick die-cut objects with a deep solid rim. They are mostly visible where cropped, and they weigh against the headline to hold the composition.
   - Evidence: `showcase/runs/felt-tip-market/0.1.0-rc.2/review-1/crops/blog--page--home--desktop-light--s1.png`, `showcase/runs/felt-tip-market/0.1.0-rc.2/review-1/crops/commerce--page--home--desktop-light--s1.png`, `showcase/runs/felt-tip-market/0.1.0-rc.2/review-1/crops/dashboard--page--home--desktop-light--s1.png`, `showcase/runs/felt-tip-market/0.1.0-rc.2/review-1/crops/blog--page--about--desktop-light--s1.png`, `showcase/runs/felt-tip-market/0.1.0-rc.2/dashboard/20261025-025002/drawings/projects-d1.png`, `showcase/runs/felt-tip-market/0.1.0-rc.2/blog/20261025-025002/drawings/home-d2.png`, `pipeline/gumroad-study/reference/set/01-desktop.png`, `pipeline/gumroad-study/reference/set/02-desktop.png`
2. **major gap from the reference**, signature, 1 of 1 sample(s)
   - Principle to fix: A drawn image in a large media box is still a sticker. Build it with the full thick construction (back, side band, face, inner strokes), never as a single outline. Keep the object at 55–70% of the box width, centred, with at least 8% clear ground on every side, so the frame never cuts it. When the box is wider than 320px, raise the outer ink stroke to 3px, so the object keeps the same visual weight it has on a card.
   - What was seen (for understanding only): On the product detail page, the item's drawing is one flat outline scaled up to fill a roughly 600px image box. Its 2px ink looks hairline-thin at that size, it has no visible back or side band, and on desktop the frame cuts the object off at the bottom. The reference's objects stay chunky die-cut stickers at every size: a heavy outline relative to the object, a solid rim, and the whole object kept inside its field, or cropped only on purpose at a band edge.
   - Evidence: `showcase/runs/felt-tip-market/0.1.0-rc.2/review-1/crops/commerce--page--products-canvas-field-tote--desktop-light--s1.png`, `showcase/runs/felt-tip-market/0.1.0-rc.2/review-1/crops/commerce--page--products-merino-crew-sweater--desktop-light--s1.png`, `showcase/runs/felt-tip-market/0.1.0-rc.2/commerce/20261025-025002/drawings/products-merino-crew-sweater-d1.png`, `pipeline/gumroad-study/reference/set/01-desktop.png`
