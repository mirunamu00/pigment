What keeps it from the reference's quality:

1. **major gap from the reference**, signature, 1 of 1 sample(s)
   - Principle to fix: Before drawing a cover, plate or empty-state piece, name its subject in one noun. Build that noun's own outline from 2–3 primitives, so a stranger could guess the subject without the title. Dots and a plain disc are accents and never the whole pictogram. The pictogram fills 60–70% of its plate or cover. Compare neighbouring covers: if two share the same main shape and layout, change the silhouette, the pose or the supporting shape, not only the hues. An empty state gets a full composition or a large glyph, never a lone dot pair.
   - What was seen (for understanding only): Item covers and spot-plate pictograms are generic and repetitive. Neighbouring covers are the same drawing with the hues swapped, some primitive stacks don't read as their subject, and plates hold two or three plain dots at about 40% of the plate. The reference's pictograms each show a distinct, readable subject built from 2–3 crescent-shaded primitives that fill 60–70% of the plate, with a sparkle tucked in.
   - Evidence: `showcase/runs/morning-shapes/0.1.0-rc.1/review-1/crops/commerce--page--home--desktop-light--s1.png`, `showcase/runs/morning-shapes/0.1.0-rc.1/review-1/sheets/commerce--drawings--home.png`, `showcase/runs/morning-shapes/0.1.0-rc.1/blog/20261026-141329/drawings/home-d5.png`, `showcase/runs/morning-shapes/0.1.0-rc.1/blog/20261026-141329/drawings/home-d9.png`, `showcase/runs/morning-shapes/0.1.0-rc.1/blog/20261026-141329/drawings/about-d3.png`, `pipeline/headspace-study/reference/set/07-desktop.png`
2. **major gap from the reference**, signature, 1 of 1 sample(s)
   - Principle to fix: Header-band shapes form one cluster in the trailing third, never scattered stickers. Start with one large crescent-shaded disc (one hue with a darker crescent of the same hue, never two flat halves). Overlap a medium second shape (a cloud, a blob or a pill) 10–20% onto its edge in a second hue. Tuck a sparkle or a dot within 8–16px of the cluster. Keep every piece fully inside the band. Vary which shapes lead and how they overlap from area to area, so no two bands share the same layout.
   - What was seen (for understanding only): Header-band art is the same three stickers (a disc, a cloud and a sparkle) spread far apart in the same places on every band and in every app. Several discs are split into two flat hues like a pie chart, and some are cut off by the band's edge. The reference composes each hero as one tight cluster: shapes overlap, sparkles are tucked against the edge, and shading is a same-hue crescent.
   - Evidence: `showcase/runs/morning-shapes/0.1.0-rc.1/review-1/crops/settings--page--settings-security--desktop-light--s1.png`, `showcase/runs/morning-shapes/0.1.0-rc.1/review-1/crops/commerce--page--home--desktop-light--s1.png`, `showcase/runs/morning-shapes/0.1.0-rc.1/review-1/crops/dashboard--page--projects--desktop-light--s1.png`, `showcase/runs/morning-shapes/0.1.0-rc.1/review-1/crops/blog--page--posts-release-notes-people-read--desktop-light--s1.png`, `pipeline/headspace-study/reference/set/02-desktop.png`, `pipeline/headspace-study/reference/set/06-desktop.png`
