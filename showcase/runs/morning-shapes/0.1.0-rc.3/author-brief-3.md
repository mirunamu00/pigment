1. **major gap from the reference**, signature, 1 of 1 sample(s)
   - The spec's words that lead to it: .ms-field__art > svg { height: 100%; min-height: var(--size-art); max-width: 100%; } … <!-- Header-band cluster, one of many: lead hexagon, overlapping pill, large star + small sparkle --> <svg viewBox="0 0 240 200">
   - Principle to fix: Size desktop header-band art by width. The art box takes about 40–45% of the band, and its SVG is `width: 100%; height: auto` in a wide viewBox of about 400×240. Build it from 4–5 pieces spread across the box: a lead shape that pictures the screen's subject in one noun (for example a key or an open door for an account screen, a group of discs for a people page, a coin stack for prices), a second-hue shape cutting into it, a 48–64px four-point star, a small sparkle and optionally a cloud. On a product's front page, prefer a vignette of the product's own UI (overlapping white cards on the band) with confetti around it. Give three sample clusters with different silhouettes, label them as structure only, and say that reusing a sample's shapes or arrangement counts as a copy.
