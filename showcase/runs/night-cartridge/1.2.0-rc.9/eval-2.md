| minor | polish | 1 of 3 | On every blog route of one sample, the one-line footer note sits to the right of the newsletter window, on the window's bottom edge, at about x 640–1130. In the reference the footer line sits below its own rule (reference p1-desktop-light.png). |
| minor | crossAppConsistency | 1 of 3 | In one sample the cart line item is a bare row on the page background: no subtle card, no hue bar and no category label.
