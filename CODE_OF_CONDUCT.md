# Code of conduct

Pigment follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive in issues, pull requests and discussions; critique work, not
people; no harassment, personal attacks or discriminatory language, in public or in private.

To report unacceptable behavior, contact the maintainer, [@mirunamu00](https://github.com/mirunamu00),
privately. Reports are kept confidential. The maintainer may edit or remove comments, issues and pull
requests, and may block anyone who doesn't follow this code.
